Super Simple Resistor Guide (No Complicated Terms)

Check which sensor you have in your hand first!

CASE 1 — MOST COMMON
Do you have a 3-PIN Sensor?
If your sensor is on a small circuit board with only 3 pins (labeled +, -, out):
🎉 NO RESISTOR NEEDED!
The resistor is already built into the sensor board.
Just plug the 3 wires directly: VCC → 3.3V, GND → GND, DATA → Pin D4.
CASE 2 — BARE SENSOR
Do you have a 4-PIN Sensor?
If your sensor is a white plastic box with 4 bare metal legs, you need the resistor to connect between the VCC wire and the DATA wire:
RED WIRE (3.3V) ───────┬─────── DHT22 Pin 1 (VCC)
10k RESISTOR (Bridge)
YELLOW WIRE (Pin D4) ───┴─────── DHT22 Pin 2 (DATA)
👉 In plain words: One leg of the resistor touches VCC (3.3V), and the other leg touches DATA (Pin D4).
