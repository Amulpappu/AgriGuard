AgriGuard IoT Node — Exact Wire Connections

ESP32 DevKit V1 + DHT22 Temp/Humidity + Capacitive Soil Moisture v1.2

🔌 Exact Wire-by-Wire Instructions

RED WIRE 1
ESP32 3V3 ➔ Breadboard (+) Rail
Connects pin 3V3 on ESP32 to the red (+) power bus rail on your breadboard. Supplies 3.3V power to all sensors.
RED WIRE 2 & 3
Breadboard (+) Rail ➔ Sensor VCC Pins
• Wire to DHT22 Pin 1 (VCC)
• Wire to Soil Sensor VCC Pin
BLACK WIRE 1
ESP32 GND ➔ Breadboard (-) Rail
Connects pin GND on ESP32 to the blue/black (-) ground bus rail on your breadboard. Creates common ground.
BLACK WIRE 2 & 3
Breadboard (-) Rail ➔ Sensor GND Pins
• Wire to DHT22 Pin 4 (GND)
• Wire to Soil Sensor GND Pin
YELLOW WIRE
DHT22 Pin 2 (DATA) ➔ ESP32 Pin D4 (GPIO 4)
Connects the digital data pin of the DHT22 directly to GPIO 4 on the ESP32. Transmits temperature & humidity telemetry.
10kΩ RESISTOR
Pull-Up: Between DHT22 DATA and 3.3V
Place a 10kΩ resistor (Brown-Black-Orange) bridging DHT22 DATA (GPIO 4) to the 3.3V (+) rail for 1-wire bus signal stability.
BLUE WIRE
Soil Sensor AOUT ➔ ESP32 Pin D34 (GPIO 34)
Connects the analog signal pin (AOUT) of the capacitive soil sensor directly to GPIO 34 (ADC1_CH6) on the ESP32. ADC1 remains 100% active while Wi-Fi transmits.

⚠️ Critical Hardware Safety Rules

1. Never use 5V on ESP32 GPIOs: ESP32 pins are 3.3V logic only. Powering DHT22 or the Soil probe from 5V could damage GPIO 4 or GPIO 34.
2. Wi-Fi ADC conflict: Always use GPIO 34 (ADC1). ADC2 channels (GPIO 0, 2, 4, 12-15, 25-27) shut down when Wi-Fi is transmitting.
