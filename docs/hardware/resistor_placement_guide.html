DHT22 10kΩ PULL-UP RESISTOR DETAIL PLACEMENT

Exact row-by-row breadboard connections between DHT22 Pin 1 (VCC) & Pin 2 (DATA)

Placement Checklist
Step 1 • Resistor Leg 1
Plug into Breadboard Row 10
Connects to the same row as DHT22 Pin 1 (VCC) and the 3.3V Power Jumper Wire.
Step 2 • Resistor Leg 2
Plug into Breadboard Row 11
Connects to the same row as DHT22 Pin 2 (DATA) and the Yellow Wire going to ESP32 Pin D4.

💡 Resistor Polarity & Role

  • No Polarity: Resistors can be inserted in either direction.
  • Color Bands: Brown • Black • Orange • Gold (10kΩ)
  • Purpose: Pulls 1-wire digital bus HIGH (3.3V) when idle so the ESP32 receives clean sensor pulses without timeouts.

⚠️ 4-Pin vs 3-Pin Sensor Check

• Bare 4-Pin DHT22: Requires this 10kΩ resistor.
• 3-Pin Module (on mini PCB): Already has a built-in SMD resistor! No external resistor needed.
